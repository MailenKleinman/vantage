About the Event

One day. One room. A room full of leaders choosing to lead themselves first.

Live2Lead brings together live, in-person content with virtual sessions from some of today's top leadership voices — all in one day, designed specifically for the leaders in this room.

This year's theme, Impact Over Impression, picks up right where Volume 1 left off: leadership isn't about how you're perceived — it's about the difference you actually make, day in and day out, for the people who follow you.

Whether you're leading a team, a department, or an entire operation, you'll leave with practical tools you can apply the next morning — not just inspiration that fades by Monday.
