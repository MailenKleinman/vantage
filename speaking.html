I spent years as the "go-to" problem solver: non-commissioned officer, business leader, and coach. Despite outward achievement, I saw what happens when leadership is just about charisma or hustle—people burn out, results fade, and true influence is lost.

My turning point was learning that real transformation doesn't come from hype or motivation alone—it comes from equipping leaders to lead themselves first.

That's why I created the IMPACT Framework™: a practical, proven process forged by decades of lessons, tested under pressure, and implemented with real teams. Today, I bring these hard-won insights to every stage and audience so your leaders can move from chaos to calm, from reaction to real, sustained results.
