About Us

Founded by Ervin Watson, a leadership strategist, keynote speaker, and creator of the IMPACT Framework™, Vantage Resource Group is built to help leaders move from reaction to sustainable impact.

Leadership Strategist & Executive Coach

Ervin Watson

From the earliest days of his career in Rochester, NY, Ervin was hooked on leadership. As a college freshman in New York, he picked up The One Minute Manager and quickly became a self-proclaimed "leadership junkie." That single book ignited a fascination with how people could be motivated, inspired, and equipped to achieve more than they ever thought possible.

His journey led him through leadership in the military and into a range of business organizations. Though he found career success, he soon realized that what truly energized him was not business, but people. His wife, his best friend for over 32 years, often joked that he was "drawn to people with problems to solve." She was right. As he invested more in others, it became clear that helping leaders grow, adapt, and become more proactive wasn't just his passion, it was his purpose. After his military career, Ervin settled in the Atlanta area (Marietta, GA), where that purpose flourished into his life's work.

25+ Years of Experience
32+ Organizations Served
1,000s Leaders Transformed

One pivotal day, a manager Ervin was mentoring asked, "Ervin, I see you pouring into others, but who mentored you?" The question stopped him in his tracks. He realized that despite his success, he never had a mentor personally invest in his professional development.

From that moment on, he made a promise: he would never let a growth-minded person cross his path without offering everything he had learned to help them along their journey. His mission is simple: leave people better than they were before, even if it's just a fleeting encounter.

Ervin believes leadership isn't about titles or hierarchy, it's about building others who can lead themselves. His signature IMPACT Framework™ and 5 A's process were born from decades of first-hand lessons, late-night leadership books, military discipline, and real-world business application.

Experience taught him that the difference between burnout and breakthrough is proactivity: helping people shift from reacting to leading, from chaos to clarity, and from firefighting to focused, meaningful impact.

Ervin loves to start every workshop with a fun fact. Here's his: in college, he was a late-night R&B and soft rock DJ, think the smooth vibes of Luther Vandross and Journey. (And yes, he's still an avid, though not always accurate, golfer.)

When he's not coaching or speaking, you'll find him chasing golf balls, spending time with his wife, three grown kids, two grandkids, or serving in his local church.
