Ervin's Story

I understand this struggle—because I've lived it.

As a college freshman in Rochester, NY, I became obsessed with leadership, inspired by The One Minute Manager. From the military to the boardroom, I chased performance, climbed the ladder, and earned every badge of achievement. But despite my success, something was missing: I was leading others, but not truly leading myself.

The turning point came when I realized that sustainable, meaningful impact isn't about working harder—it's about becoming the kind of leader who is proactive, purposeful, and aligned from within.

That's why I created the IMPACT Framework™ and built Vantage Resource Group—to help leaders like you move from overwhelmed to empowered, from reactive to intentional, and from chaos to real, lasting impact.

IMPACT™ over Impression

Intentionality: "Impact doesn't happen by chance—it's crafted by conscious choice." Leaders who are deliberate with decisions and actions turn vision into reality, creating sustainable success rather than leaving results up to fate.
Mindset: "A leader's greatest asset isn't just their answers—it's their attitude." By cultivating resilience, curiosity, and optimism, you equip yourself and your team to navigate uncertainty and challenge with confidence.
Purpose: "Purpose isn't a hat you wear; it's the roots that anchor your life and leadership." When your "why" is clear and deeply rooted, every action is fueled by meaning, driving commitment and inspiring those you lead.
Accountability: "Accountability is the discipline of measuring what you did—not what you hoped." True progress comes from tracking results, owning outcomes, and closing the gap between intention and execution.
Connection: "You cannot make an IMPACT without first making Contact." Real leadership begins with genuine relationships—because sustainable influence is built on trust, empathy, and meaningful engagement at every level.
Transformation: "Transformation is NOT a team sport"—it starts with you. The most lasting change happens from the inside out, as leaders commit to their own growth before asking it of others.
